The Warriors

Two warriors. Two philosophies. One arena. The first chapter of a universe that has no end.

01
Pandava · Warrior of Dharma
ARJUNA
अर्जुन
Speed Fighter

Born to Kunti by the blessing of Indra, Arjuna was given something rarer than strength — absolute clarity of eye and purpose. His arrow never sought what it could not find.

At Kurukshetra, he hesitated. Then Krishna spoke, and hesitation became history. Arjuna became the instrument through which dharma restored itself — not with rage, but with cold precision.

In steel: speed over mass. Every servo calibrated for rapid, cascading strikes that overwhelm before the opponent can read the pattern.

02
Pandava · Strength of the Age
BHIM
भीम
Power Destroyer

Son of Vayu — the wind god who breathes life into all things. Bhim carried that wind in his arms: deceptively fast for his size, catastrophic when it landed. Vyasa gave him the measure of ten thousand elephants and called it approximate.

When Bhim walked into Kurukshetra, the earth remembered him from before this birth. He killed the Kauravas one by one, across eighteen days, until Duryodhana's thighs broke under the Gada and the war was over.

In steel: mass over finesse. Forward pressure. Relentless advance. Every servo tuned for sustained maximum-load output — built to end fights, not extend them.
